QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is VYBE free?

+

Yes, completely. VYBE is open-source and free to use without any ads.

Does VYBE require an account?

+

No! VYBE works without an account. However, you can connect a YouTube Music account for personalization and synchronization if you want.

What Android version is required?

+

VYBE requires Android 8.0 (API 26) or above.
