One music app. Many ways to listen.

Feature set

Designed around the whole listening journey.

Discover & search

Search songs, albums, movies and artists through the YouTube Music catalog. Explore latest releases, Quick Picks and recommendations without needing to sign in.

›

YouTube Music connection

Connect an account for playlists, liked music, listening history and personalized discovery while keeping the app usable without an account.

›

Unified library

Browse downloads, songs, albums, artists, playlists, folders and liked music from one library, including music already stored on the device.

›

Offline downloads

Download supported tracks, view progress in-app and through Android notifications, then play your downloaded music offline.

›

Synced lyrics

Load synchronized lyrics automatically when available, follow the current line and optionally edit, translate or romanize lyrics with your configured AI provider.

›

Playback & queue

Background playback, Android media controls, queue management, shuffle, repeat, crossfade and configurable notification actions.

›

Material You personalization

Light, dark and system themes, dynamic color support, customizable navigation, corners, artwork quality, carousel style and playback behavior.

›

History & statistics

Review listening history and statistics for songs, albums, artists and genres, and generate a Daily Mix using your own configured AI provider.

›
